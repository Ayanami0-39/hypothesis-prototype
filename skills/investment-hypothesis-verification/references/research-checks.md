# Checks to select according to the claim

## Formalization and measurement

Write the intended construct before selecting the easiest available variable. Record
population, units, observation window, comparison and decision criterion separately.
Revenue, retention, market power and shareholder value are distinct targets. Leave
unavailable fields unresolved rather than filling them with a convenient substitute.

Look for changing reporting definitions, numerator/denominator mismatch, cumulative
versus period measures, stock versus flow, nominal versus real values, consolidation
scope, self-selection, and revised data. Point-in-time availability follows the source
vintage actually accessed, not the date the economic event happened. A present-day
restatement does not become eligible merely because it describes a historical period.

For ambiguous operationalizations retain justified alternatives and report sensitivity.
Do not treat a rounding envelope or scenario range as a confidence interval. LLM scores
need a specified rubric and validation before they can serve as measurements.

## Causality and competing explanations

For causal claims specify treatment, outcome, population, counterfactual and design.
Check selection into treatment, concurrent events, spillovers and measurement changes.
Random assignment identifies the assigned policy effect, not necessarily the effect
of voluntary uptake. Difference-in-differences needs a defensible comparison and
parallel-trend reasoning; a nonsignificant pre-trend test alone cannot establish it.

Do not infer non-inferiority from a nonsignificant difference. Declare an economically
defensible margin and use an appropriate interval/test. Partial identification or
sensitivity analysis can be more honest than an unjustified point estimate.

The next evidence request should discriminate among explanations. Examples include
recomputing a shared denominator, matching cohorts, checking a contract, separating
source revisions, and retrieving an unaffected comparison population. If no feasible
action can resolve an important gap, state the gap and stop that branch.

## Synthesis and feedback

Shared upstream data and assumptions must remain visible. Different paths can address
different propositions and should not be averaged as estimates of one parameter.
Record exactly what new evidence changes and which conclusions survive unchanged.

Keep operation checks, semantic judgments and delayed forecast outcomes separate.
Executable validation only establishes the checked operation conditional on its inputs.
Semantic labels need independent review; do not use a model's self-confidence as a
reliability weight. A correct outcome does not validate every preceding reasoning step.

An exploratory case may expose a useful failure mode but does not demonstrate general
agent quality. For a later experiment, compare shared information and budgets, preserve
failed runs, and group repetitions/edits by base case rather than inflate sample size.
